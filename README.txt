# 🏫 BSU Service Listing Website

Welcome to the **BSU Service Listing Website**, an informative platform designed to help freshmen and prospective students explore the facilities, services, and campus details of **Bulacan State University (BSU) - Arasof Campus**. This website is built using **HTML** and **CSS** to provide a simple and user-friendly interface.

---

## 🌟 Project Purpose

The **BSU Service Listing Website** aims to:
- Inform students about the facilities available at BSU-Arasof.
- Provide an interactive map for easy navigation around the campus.
- Highlight the key services offered by the university, making it easier for freshmen to adapt.
- Encourage prospective students to enroll by showcasing the benefits of studying at BSU.

---

## 🏢 Features

### 📝 1. List of Facilities
The website includes a comprehensive list of BSU-Arasof facilities, such as:
- 🏫 Academic Buildings
- 📚 Library
- 💻 Computer Labs
- 🏋️ Gym and Sports Facilities
- 🍴 Canteen and Food Stalls
- 🛏️ Dormitories
- 🚑 Clinic and Health Services
- 🚌 Transportation Services

### 🗺️ 2. Interactive Campus Map
- **Purpose**: To help students navigate the campus easily.
- Features:
  - Highlights major buildings and their purposes.
  - A "You Are Here" feature for location tracking.

### 📖 3. University Information
The website provides:
- **History of BSU-Arasof**: Learn about the university's legacy.
- **Mission and Vision**: Understand BSU's goals and values.
- **Enrollment Information**: A step-by-step guide for incoming freshmen.

### ✨ 4. Responsive Design
- Built with modern **CSS techniques**, ensuring compatibility with mobile and desktop devices.
- Offers an intuitive browsing experience for all users.
